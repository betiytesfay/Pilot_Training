# Pilot Training Curriculum
## Module 1: Aerodynamics & Principles of Flight
- Lift, Drag, Thrust, and Weight flight forces
- Stall mechanics, angle of attack, and recovery procedures
## Module 2: Aircraft Systems & Avionics
- Pitot-static instruments and modern glass cockpit avionics
## Module 3: Aviation Meteorology
- Atmospheric pressure, frontal systems, and weather briefings
